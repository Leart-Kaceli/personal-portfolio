# Personal Portfolio Project Plan

## Purpose

My portfolio will present my software projects, technical skills, problem-solving experience, and growth as a high school developer. Its main purpose is to help college admissions officers, scholarship reviewers, teachers, mentors, and potential internship recruiters quickly understand what I have built and what I learned from building it.


## Main goal

The main goal of this website is to show that I can take a real problem, design a solution, build a full-stack application, test it, deploy it, and improve it based on feedback.


## Audience

The portfolio is designed for:

1. College admissions officers
2. Scholarship reviewers
3. Internship recruiters
4. Teachers and mentors
5. Other students and developers




### College admissions officers

They should quickly understand that I created a serious technical project outside of normal classwork and followed it from idea to production.

### Scholarship reviewers

They should see initiative, independence, problem-solving, and a project intended to help other students.

### Internship recruiters

They should see the technologies I used, the technical decisions I made, and the engineering challenges I solved.

### Teachers and mentors

They should be able to review my work and recommend me based on a concrete project.

### Other students and developers

They should be able to try the live project, inspect the repository, and provide feedback.


## Website pages

The launch version of the portfolio will include:

- Home
- About
- Projects
- AP Path Planner case study
- Contact


### Home

The homepage will introduce me, summarize my interests, feature AP Path Planner, list my main technical skills, and direct visitors to my work.

### About

The About page will explain how I became interested in software development, what I enjoy building, what I learned from AP Path Planner, and what I hope to study.

### Projects

The Projects page will display my strongest work in a clean, easy-to-scan format.

### AP Path Planner case study

The case study will explain the problem, solution, features, architecture, testing, challenges, lessons learned, and future plans.

### Contact

The Contact page will provide a safe and professional way to reach me through email and GitHub.


## Launch content

The portfolio will launch with one fully documented featured project:

- AP Path Planner

## Visual direction

The website will use a clean, professional, modern style.

### Colors

- Background: white or very light slate
- Main text: dark navy or slate
- Secondary text: medium slate
- Accent: blue
- Borders: light slate
- Dark mode: optional after the main version is complete

### Design style

- Rounded cards
- Subtle shadows
- Clear spacing
- Large readable headings
- Minimal animation
- Strong mobile responsiveness
- Visible keyboard focus states


Background: slate-50
Cards: white
Primary text: slate-950
Secondary text: slate-600
Accent: blue-600
Accent hover: blue-700
Borders: slate-200


### Design choices to avoid

- Neon colors across the entire page
- Constant moving animations
- Skill bars with fake percentages
- Background music
- Huge walls of text
- Too many gradients
- 3D effects that slow the site down
- Very small text
- Low-contrast gray text
